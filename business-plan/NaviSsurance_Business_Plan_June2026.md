- The founder has deep domain expertise + the tool. The missing piece has been systematic execution on bizdev and capacity — both of which Navi itself can now drive.

## 1. Company & Vision

### Current State
NaviSure Consulting is a solo-founder MedTech regulatory practice (AI/ML SaMD, IVD/LDT focus) that has built a sophisticated internal AI operating system (NaviSsurance) to support its own work. The practice is profitable at low overhead but faces classic solo constraints: high client concentration, limited bizdev bandwidth, and the need to increase capacity without proportional time input.

### Vision (Aligned with Approved Product Roadmap)
NaviSsurance becomes a single, intelligent workspace that functions like a small, highly competent team working exclusively for the consultant. It maintains deep memory of clients and past work, proactively monitors the regulatory and competitive environment, helps plan and prioritize, captures time and revenue cleanly, and dramatically accelerates high-quality client deliverables by intelligently reusing real historical work.

This plan operationalizes that vision for business growth: use the tool internally first to 2–3x effective capacity and solve the founder's stated execution gaps, then (only after proof) consider productization as a secondary revenue stream.

## 3. Market Opportunity

MedTech regulatory consulting sits at the intersection of two large, growing markets:
- Global medical devices ~$572B+ (2025), growing ~7%.
- IVD ~$109B, growing ~7.6%.
- Regulatory consulting rates typically $275–450/hr, with project fees commonly $18k–$50k+.

Demand drivers (AI SaMD, increasing regulatory scrutiny, sponsor preference for experienced specialists) create tailwinds for high-value, specialized practices. At the same time, these practices are often solo or small and suffer exactly the administrative, memory, and capacity problems NaviSsurance is designed to solve.

The addressable market for a vertical AI "consultant OS" in this niche is real but secondary to the primary strategy: using the tool to grow a high-quality services practice first.

## 4. Business Model & Pricing (Services-First)

**Primary model (per founder direction):** 100% services revenue target of $500k in 36 months.

- Continue and grow high-value fractional + project work in AI/ML SaMD, IVD, and related regulatory work.
- Use NaviSsurance internally to increase effective capacity and quality (20–30%+ utilization lift once Phase 4 matures).
- Position as "Navi-powered" for speed, consistency, and memory of prior work — a differentiator that supports premium positioning or faster delivery at existing rates.

**Secondary / future (explicitly not required for the $500k goal):**
- Desktop / hybrid subscription for peer solo consultants and small boutiques ($199/mo Solo or $4,999/yr Firm as test pricing, benchmarked against Harvey and vertical SaaS).
- Onboarding + template customization services as a high-margin upsell.
- Alpha/pilot only after internal proof and 1–2 peer case studies.

Pricing and packaging for any product offering will be tested only after the services base is stronger and the app demonstrably saves the founder meaningful time.

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy

**Phase 1 (0–6 months):** "Use the product to sell the services."
- Build and run the entire bizdev system inside Navi (CoS templates for outreach sequences, Intel watchlists for regulatory triggers, Workspace for proposal generation using real past work, full tracking in Clients/Tasks).
- Target 8–12 outreaches and 2–3 proposals per month.
- Use live demos of the app in pitches as a killer differentiator.
- Produce and publish 2 case studies using the app's own workflows.

**Phase 2 (6–18 months):** Proof + light product exploration.
- Once 1–2 new clients are won and Phase 4 is delivering measurable lift, consider a very small closed alpha with 3–5 peer solos.
- No public product launch or heavy marketing spend until internal value is proven.

**Phase 3 (18+ months):** Only if justified by data.
- Decide on formal productization, pricing, and channels based on real usage and willingness-to-pay data.

All early GTM is deliberately low-cost and high-signal, run inside the tool itself.

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

1. **Dova Concentration (HIGH)**  
   Mitigation: Entire 0–6 month plan is Navi-powered bizdev + explicit diversification gate before any hiring or major spend.

2. **App Not Yet Delivering Time Savings (MED-HIGH)**  
   Mitigation: Phase 4 is #1 product priority. All projections are conservative on lift timing. Founder will measure actual hours saved inside the app before scaling outreach volume.

3. **Bizdev Execution / "No Time, No Strategy" (HIGH)**  
   Mitigation: Force the entire system inside existing Navi surfaces (CoS templates, Intel triggers, Workspace proposals, tracking). The 90-day plan includes explicit Day 1 setup time-box (30–60 min) to remove friction.

4. **Founder Velocity / Solo Bandwidth**  
   Mitigation: 90-day plan is deliberately scoped; conservative scenario assumes only 1 new client per year; weekly CoS review of the plan itself creates accountability.

5. **Productization Distraction**  
   Mitigation: Explicitly deprioritized until services base is stronger and internal value is proven. "App sales would be a bonus."
