## 1. Introduction

NaviSsurance is an AI-powered Chief of Staff and specialist team platform designed to help Dr. Adam Odeh manage complex regulatory consulting work with greater efficiency and less cognitive load.

---

## 2. User Stories

### Memory & Knowledge Management

**US-001**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want Navi to automatically remember important details from conversations so that I don’t have to repeat myself across sessions.

**US-002**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to explicitly teach Navi information using natural language so that I can quickly add client-specific or project-specific knowledge.

**US-003**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to review, edit, and delete memories so that I can maintain accuracy and control over what Navi knows.

**US-004**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want memories to be associated with specific clients or projects when relevant so that the right context is available when needed.

**US-005**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want meeting transcriptions to be automatically summarized and stored in long-term memory so that key discussion points are retained without manual effort.

**US-006**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want meeting summaries to be automatically associated with the relevant client (when identifiable) so that client-specific knowledge is properly organized.

---

### Chief of Staff & Daily Planning

**US-010**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want Navi to act as my Chief of Staff by helping me plan and prioritize my day so that I can reduce decision fatigue.

**US-011**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want Navi to automatically generate a daily plan every morning by reviewing my tasks, calendar, emails, and relevant news.

**US-012**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want Navi to consider my energy levels and preferred working style (stored in memory) when creating the daily schedule.

**US-013**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want the suggested daily schedule to be presented in a calendar-style layout (similar to Google Calendar’s single-day view).

**US-014**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to review and adjust Navi’s suggested schedule before it is finalized.

**US-015**  
As Dr. Odeh, once I approve the daily plan, I want the system to automatically add time blocks to my calendar and trigger the appropriate specialist agents.

---

### Workspace & Document Generation

**US-020**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to generate high-quality regulatory documents quickly from source materials.

**US-021**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want the Workspace to use a dual-LLM process (Grok generates → ChatGPT reviews) to improve quality.

**US-022**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to use templates for common document types so that outputs are consistent and professional.

**US-023**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want the system to identify missing information or research gaps during document generation.

---

### Task & Assignment Management

**US-030**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want a clear system to create, track, and manage tasks and assignments.

**US-031**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want Navi to automatically extract actionable tasks from conversations and meeting transcripts.

**US-032**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to delegate work to specialist agents and track their progress.

---

### Client Management (Planned)

**US-040**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want a dedicated Client Dossier that shows key facts, active work, documents, and recent activity for each client.

---

### Automation & Operations

**US-050**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want recurring administrative tasks (such as monthly billing) to be handled automatically.

**US-051**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want the system to run reliably in the background and notify me when action is needed.

---

### Billing

**US-060**  
As Dr. Odeh, I want to generate accurate and professional invoices efficiently.

---

## 3. Non-Functional Goals

- The system should feel fast and responsive.
- I should remain in control of what the AI does and remembers.
- The system should be reliable across sessions and restarts.
