**Executive Summary (June 2026)**

NaviSure Consulting (Dr. Adam Odeh) is a specialized MedTech regulatory practice (AI/ML SaMD, IVD/LDT focus) at ~$230k run-rate (last 12mo, 90% from one high-value fractional VP client + 4 others). Solo founder at 70% billable / 25% app / 5% bizdev, very low overhead ($400/mo), comfortable bootstrap.

The core asset and growth lever is **NaviSsurance (Consultant OS)** — a desktop-first, local-first AI platform that functions as a "small, highly competent team" (per the approved vision in `consultant-os-roadmap.md`). Deep layered memory, proactive Intel (Pulse), Chief of Staff planning/delegation, Workspace with real historical work reuse (Phase 4 advancing), native billing, evidence-first leads, compliance, client dossiers.

**Current state (June 2026, per founder + `roadmap_status.md`):** Phase 1 (Memory & Retrieval) 100% complete; Intel & Coordination (roadmap Phase 3) + Client Dossier advanced; Phase 4 partial (~25%, auto-injection of strong historical matches live and already valuable). The app is not yet delivering measurable time savings vs plain Grok for the founder.

**Founder-directed goal:** $500k total revenue in 36 months, 100% from consulting services. App sales treated as secondary/unknown "bonus." Use Navi primarily as an internal operating system / staff replacement lever to:
- Solve "no time and no strategy for bizdev" (the #1 blocker) by running systematic outreach and proposals inside the app.
- Deliver 20–30%+ capacity lift via faster, higher-quality deliverables that reuse real past work.
- Free founder time for high-value FDA/client work + personal/family life.
- Enable the first justified hire once the client base supports it.

**Key numbers (Base, 36 months)**
- Revenue: $230k → $500k+ services
- Clients: 5 (concentrated) → 12–14 diversified
- Gross margin: ~78%
- LTV:CAC: >30x
- First hire: ~month 18 (revenue-triggered only)

**Immediate 90-Day Actions (start Monday, all inside Navi)**
1. Prioritize Phase 4 Workspace completion for first measurable time savings.
2. Build and run bizdev system inside CoS + Intel + Workspace (templates, watchlists, proposals, tracking). Target 8–12 outreaches/mo and 2–3 proposals.
3. Produce 2 case studies using the app's own workflows.
4. Review the full plan + financial model; decide on any small loan for conferences once traction appears.
5. Pilot app alpha with 1–2 peer solos (internal validation only).
6. Weekly Chief of Staff review of this plan.

**Top Risks**
- Dova concentration (HIGH) → Diversification is the entire near-term focus.
- App not yet saving time (MED-HIGH) → Phase 4 is #1 priority; measure lift in-app.
- No current bizdev process (HIGH) → Force the system inside existing Navi surfaces.
